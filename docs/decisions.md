# Decisions made

1. **No single goal yet.** Study the shared core (Quant, Reasoning, English, GK) daily; placements first, then defence + GATE in final year, then SSC/IBPS; UPSC only as a full-time decision.
2. **Official syllabi only, nothing made up.** Topic lists follow each exam's notification (SSC, UPSC CDS/CSE, AFCAT, GATE 2027 by IIT Madras). IBPS publishes no chapter list, so its topics come from its section pattern plus recent papers, and the app says so. The placement list is labelled as a plan, not an official syllabus.
3. **Legal sources only.** FMHY was used for free education and cyber links, but its links to pirated coaching videos and books were left out. Anna's Archive and other pirate libraries were declined, even for offline personal study. Free author-released books, NCERT/NIOS/government PDFs, and a buy-or-borrow list are used instead.
4. **Real past-paper questions only.** No questions typed from memory. Real questions come in by importing official question-paper PDFs into the app (or saving them by hand), plus a PYQ counter and source links on every topic.
5. **GATE CSE added** (paper code CS). GATE 2027 as a practice attempt in 3rd year, GATE 2028 as the real one.
6. **Engagement features:** rank ladder, mission briefing, streak shield, boss battles, weak-spot radar, speed ladder, bus mode with listen-aloud, cyber quest map, daily CTF flag, Sunday debrief, "your why" letter.
7. **Library downloaded locally:** 395 PDFs (1.45 GB) in `C:\Users\tamil\Documents\INStudy_Library`. NCERT's retired Class 9 social science books were replaced by the new *Understanding Society: India and Beyond*.
8. **This repo holds code and docs, not the PDFs.** The PDFs are too large for Git; `docs/library-contents.md` lists them and `study-library/get_study_pdfs.py` re-downloads them.
