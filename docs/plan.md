# The plan

For Inbathamizhan S: B.Tech CSE (Cyber Security), 3rd year, Kalasalingam University. Goals under consideration: defence officer (AFCAT, CDS), a high-paying company job, central govt jobs (SSC CGL, IBPS PO), GATE CSE, and possibly UPSC.

## One engine, many exits

You don't have to choose one goal now. SSC CGL, IBPS PO, AFCAT, CDS and the UPSC CSAT paper all test the same core: **Quant, Reasoning, English and GK**. Study that core every day and you prepare for all of them at once. GATE CSE shares OS, DBMS, networks and DSA with placements.

## Order of goals

1. **Now until 7th sem:** placements are the main goal (DSA, cyber skills, 2 solid projects). One aptitude block every day alongside.
2. **Final year:** apply for AFCAT and CDS (final-year students are allowed), start SSB prep, and take GATE 2028 as the real attempt (GATE 2027 in 3rd year as a practice attempt).
3. **After graduation:** SSC CGL and IBPS PO.
4. **UPSC:** only if you can commit 1 to 2 years full-time. Decide after final year.

## Daily routine (daily bus commute)

- **Bus to college:** flashcards and vocab (Bus mode in the app). No maths on a moving bus.
- **Bus back:** sleep, or current affairs / Listen mode.
- **At home:** eat, then nap for 25 minutes only.
- **Evening:** two 50-minute blocks. Block A = aptitude, rotating through the week (the app picks your weakest area). Block B = cyber / placement / GATE.
- **Before bed:** 20 minutes of revision, sleep by 11.
- **Sunday:** one full mock test, then the Sunday debrief.
