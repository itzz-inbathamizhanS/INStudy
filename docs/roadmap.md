# Roadmap: zero to exam-ready

The same 31 steps as the Roadmap tab in Study Desk. Each step names the book or free resource, what to cover, and which exams it helps.

## Stage 1 · Foundation

**When:** Nov 2026 to Jan 2027. Zero to basics. Everything later stands on this.

### 1. NCERT Maths, Class 6 to 10

*Free · ncert.nic.in* · helps: All exams

Fractions, percentages, ratio, algebra, geometry and mensuration from scratch. Skim what you know, solve every exercise you don’t.

Study list, in order:

1. Numbers: place value, large numbers, estimation (Class 6)
2. Whole numbers and integers, operations on integers (Class 6 to 7)
3. Factors, multiples, divisibility rules, prime numbers (Class 6)
4. HCF and LCM (Class 6, again in Class 10)
5. Fractions and decimals (Class 6 to 7)
6. Ratio, proportion, unitary method (Class 6 to 8)
7. Percentage, profit and loss, simple interest (Class 7 to 8)
8. Compound interest (Class 8)
9. Exponents and powers (Class 7 to 8)
10. Squares, square roots, cubes, cube roots (Class 8)
11. Algebraic expressions and identities (Class 7 to 8)
12. Linear equations in one variable (Class 7 to 8)
13. Number systems: rational and irrational numbers (Class 9)
14. Polynomials, factorisation (Class 9 to 10)
15. Linear equations in two variables, pair of equations (Class 9 to 10)
16. Quadratic equations (Class 10)
17. Arithmetic progressions (Class 10)
18. Lines and angles, parallel lines (Class 6 to 9)
19. Triangles: properties, congruence (Class 7 to 9)
20. Similar triangles, Pythagoras theorem (Class 7 to 10)
21. Quadrilaterals and parallelograms (Class 8 to 9)
22. Circles: chords, tangents (Class 9 to 10)
23. Coordinate geometry (Class 9 to 10)
24. Perimeter and area of 2D shapes, Heron’s formula (Class 6 to 9)
25. Surface area and volume of solids (Class 8 to 10)
26. Trigonometry: ratios and identities (Class 10)
27. Heights and distances (Class 10)
28. Statistics: mean, median, mode, bar graphs, histograms (Class 6 to 10)
29. Probability (Class 9 to 10)

### 6. Striver A2Z DSA sheet, steps 1 to 3

*Free · takeuforward.org* · helps: Placement · GATE

Basics, sorting and arrays in one language (C++ or Java). One topic a day in Block B.

Study list, in order:

1. Basics: input/output, data types, conditions, loops
2. Functions and pass by value/reference
3. Basic maths: digits, GCD, primes, divisors
4. Basic recursion
5. Basic hashing (counting frequencies)
6. Sorting I: selection, bubble, insertion
7. Sorting II: merge sort, quick sort
8. Arrays: easy problems
9. Arrays: medium problems
10. Arrays: hard problems

### 8. OSTEP (Operating Systems)

*Free book* · helps: GATE · Placement

Virtualisation, concurrency, persistence. Pair with your OS semester course.

Study list, in order:

1. Processes and the process API
2. CPU scheduling
3. Address spaces and memory API
4. Segmentation and paging
5. TLBs and multi-level page tables
6. Swapping and page replacement
7. Threads and locks
8. Condition variables and semaphores
9. Concurrency bugs and deadlock
10. I/O devices and disks
11. File systems
12. Crash consistency and journaling

### 9. Korth, Database System Concepts

*Book* · helps: GATE · Placement

ER model, relational algebra, SQL, normalisation, transactions, indexing.

Study list, in order:

1. ER model
2. Relational model
3. Relational algebra and tuple calculus
4. SQL: joins, grouping, subqueries
5. Integrity constraints
6. Functional dependencies
7. Normal forms: 1NF to BCNF
8. File organisation and indexing, B and B+ trees
9. Transactions and ACID
10. Concurrency control: serialisability, 2PL
11. Recovery

### 10. Kurose & Ross, Computer Networking

*Book* · helps: GATE · Placement · Cyber

Top-down: application, transport, network, link layer.

Study list, in order:

1. Layering: OSI and TCP/IP
2. Application layer: HTTP, DNS, email
3. Transport layer: UDP, TCP, reliable transfer
4. Flow control and congestion control
5. Network layer: IPv4, subnetting, CIDR, NAT
6. Routing: distance vector, link state
7. Link layer: framing, error detection, MAC, Ethernet
8. Switching: packet, circuit, virtual circuit
9. Network security basics

1. Discrete maths: logic, sets, relations, groups
2. Discrete maths: graphs, combinatorics, recurrences
3. Linear algebra and calculus
4. Probability and statistics
5. Digital logic
6. Computer organisation and architecture
7. Algorithms (CLRS)
8. Theory of computation (Linz)
9. Compiler design (Aho and Ullman)
10. Revise OS, DBMS, networks from Stage 2

*Free* · helps: GATE
