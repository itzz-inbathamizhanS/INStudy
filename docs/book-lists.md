# Book lists

No single book is enough by itself. What works for every exam is: core book, then previous-year papers, then mock tests. Where a book appears for more than one exam, buy it once and reuse it.

For the full buy-or-borrow list with prices and the free downloads, see [study-library/README.md](../study-library/README.md). For the order to read them in, see [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## SSC CGL
- Quant: Rakesh Yadav, *Class Notes* (Arithmetic, then Advance Maths)
- Reasoning: R.S. Aggarwal, *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning*
- English: Neetu Singh, *Plinth to Paramount*
- GK and computer: *Lucent's General Knowledge* and *Lucent's Computer*
- Kiran SSC CGL previous year papers

## IBPS PO
- Quant and DI: Arun Sharma, *Quantitative Aptitude*
- Reasoning: M.K. Pandey, *Analytical Reasoning*
- English: S.P. Bakshi, *Objective General English*
- Banking awareness: RBI and PIB updates (free) plus a monthly current affairs PDF

## AFCAT and CDS
- Arihant *Pathfinder* (one for AFCAT, one for CDS)
- Maths: NCERT Class 6 to 10 (free)
- SSB: Dr. N.K. Natarajan, *SSB Interview: The Complete Guide*

## UPSC Prelims
- NCERT Class 6 to 12 (free)
- Polity: Laxmikanth · Modern History: Spectrum (Rajiv Ahir) · Geography: G.C. Leong · Economy: Ramesh Singh · Environment: Shankar IAS · Art and Culture: Nitin Singhania
- UPSC past papers (free on upsc.gov.in)

## GATE CSE
- Algorithms: CLRS · OS: Galvin (or free OSTEP) · DBMS: Korth · Networks: Kurose & Ross · TOC: Peter Linz · Compilers: Aho & Ullman · COA: Hamacher · Digital Logic: Morris Mano · Discrete Maths: Kenneth Rosen
- GATE Overflow past questions and NPTEL lectures (free)

## Placements
- Striver's A2Z DSA sheet (free, takeuforward.org)
- *Cracking the Coding Interview* (Gayle McDowell)
- Aptitude: R.S. Aggarwal *Quantitative Aptitude* or IndiaBIX (free)

## Free, legal full textbooks
- OS: *Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces* (OSTEP)
- Networks: *Computer Networks: A Systems Approach*
- Algorithms: Jeff Erickson, *Algorithms*; Pat Morin, *Open Data Structures*
- Discrete maths: MIT *Mathematics for Computer Science*; Probability: Grinstead & Snell
- TOC: Maheshwari & Smid; Compilers: Wirth, *Compiler Construction*
- Security: Ross Anderson, *Security Engineering*; *The Linux Command Line*; *Automate the Boring Stuff with Python*
- GK / UPSC / IBPS: NIOS study material, official Constitution text, Union Budget and Economic Survey

**If buying, start with:** Lucent's GK, R.S. Aggarwal Reasoning and Laxmikanth. Together they cover the most exams.
